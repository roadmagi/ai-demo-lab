# Billing and plans

Kestrel has three plans. All prices are per seat, per month, billed in USD.

## Plan comparison

| | Solo | Team | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price (monthly billing) | $9 | $18 | $34 |
| Price (annual billing) | $7.50 | $15 | $28 |
| Included seats | 1 | 3 minimum | 10 minimum |
| Projects | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Guest collaborators | Not available | 10 | Unlimited |
| Priority support | No | No | Yes, 4-hour first response |

Annual billing is charged once up front and works out to roughly two months
free compared to paying monthly. You can switch from monthly to annual at any
time; switching from annual back to monthly takes effect at your next renewal
date rather than immediately.

## Adding and removing seats

Seats can be added at any point in a billing period. When you add a seat
mid-cycle we charge a prorated amount for the remaining days, calculated to the
day and added to your next invoice rather than charged immediately.

Removing a seat does not generate a refund. Instead the seat stays active until
the end of the current billing period, and your next invoice is reduced. If you
remove a seat and add a different one in the same period, the two cancel out
and you are not charged twice.

Workspace owners and billing admins can change seat counts. Regular members
cannot, and will see the seat controls greyed out.

## Invoices and payment methods

Invoices are generated on your renewal date and emailed to every billing admin
on the workspace. You can also download any past invoice as a PDF from
**Settings → Billing → Invoice history**. Invoices there go back to the start of
the workspace and are never purged.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Business plan customers who
need to pay by bank transfer can request invoicing terms by contacting support;
we require an annual commitment for bank transfer billing.

If a payment fails we retry three times over ten days and email the billing
admins each time. After the third failure the workspace moves to a read-only
state rather than being deleted — see below.

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel from **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Cancellation takes
effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep full access until
the date shown on the confirmation screen. We do not refund partial periods.

When the period ends, the workspace becomes **read-only**. In read-only state:

- Everyone can still sign in, view every project, and read all comments
- Nobody can create, edit, move, or delete anything
- Integrations stop sending updates
- Automations stop running
- Exports still work, so you can take your data out at any time

Read-only workspaces are kept for **90 days**. During that window, resubscribing
restores full access immediately with everything intact — no data is lost and
nothing needs to be re-imported.

After 90 days the workspace and all of its data are permanently deleted from our
production systems, and from backups within a further 30 days. This deletion is
irreversible. We email every workspace owner at 60 days, 14 days, and 24 hours
before deletion so the deadline is not a surprise.

If you want to leave before your period ends, cancel and then export your data
straight away — cancelling does not disable exports.
